# Mandatory Financial Guidance Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every finance-related skill, workflow, calculation, recommendation, document, and generated output. It cannot be overridden by domain context or a user-specific allocation policy.

## Nature of guidance

- Ethan OS does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice.
- Financial outputs are educational planning assistance generated from user-provided information, general financial-planning principles, commonly used methodologies, publicly available knowledge, and widely discussed or crowdsourced approaches.
- Recommendations are possible strategies, not instructions, guarantees, fiduciary advice, or claims that one choice is universally correct.
- Outcomes are uncertain and depend on individual circumstances, taxes, laws, market conditions, interest rates, employment, risk tolerance, account terms, and information the system may not know.
- The user remains responsible for decisions and should independently verify important calculations, contribution limits, tax implications, account rules, eligibility requirements, and other time-sensitive information.
- Recommend review by an appropriately qualified professional when a decision is high-impact, complex, tax-sensitive, or legally significant.

## Recommendation language

Use qualified, decision-support language such as:

- "Based on the information currently available..."
- "One reasonable approach would be..."
- "Under the selected debt-payoff methodology..."
- "This appears to be the higher-priority use of available cash because..."
- "Consider..."

Do not present financial recommendations as authoritative commands or guarantees. Avoid wording such as:

- "You must..."
- "You should definitely..."
- "This is guaranteed..."
- "This is the correct financial decision..."

Operational requirements and data-integrity rules may still use mandatory language; this restriction applies to recommendations about the user's financial choices.

## Explainability and methodology

Every material recommendation must:

1. Explain the reasoning in terms the user can evaluate.
2. Identify the methodology and material assumptions driving it.
3. Cite the dated user facts and calculated values it relies on.
4. State material missing or stale information that could change the result.
5. Present meaningful tradeoffs when reasonable methodologies disagree.

Examples include:

- Debt avalanche prioritizes higher effective APR and generally seeks lower interest cost.
- Debt snowball prioritizes smaller balances and may provide earlier visible wins.
- Promotional-expiration-aware payoff considers rate-reset timing and the post-promotion APR.
- Safety-reserve targets use estimated essential and committed monthly expenses and an identified coverage period.
- Retirement contributions use the selected annual target, year-to-date contributions, remaining pay periods, and dated plan or legal limits.
- Investment suggestions may use commonly discussed diversification, liquidity, time-horizon, and risk-management principles; they must not imply guaranteed returns.

## Credit limit and available credit

- Credit limits are stored solely for credit utilization ratio calculations (e.g. credit score health metrics).
- Available credit (credit limit minus balance) must never be presented as available funds, spending capacity, or a financial resource.
- Do not suggest, imply, or frame unused credit as money the user can or should spend.
- Utilization ratios may be surfaced in snapshots and credit health summaries as analytical metrics only.

## Disclaimer prominence

- Do not clutter routine factual capture, balance updates, or low-impact calculations with a large repeated disclaimer.
- Normal recommendations may include a short notice or policy reference when appropriate.
- Substantial, high-impact, complex, tax-sensitive, investment-specific, or legally significant recommendations must prominently state that the output is educational planning assistance, identify verification needs, and suggest qualified professional review when warranted.
