# Knowledge & Learning

## What it does

Capture what you learn from any source — books, articles, podcasts, videos, courses, conversations, or experience — and turn it into durable, connected ideas you can actually use.

## Why it exists

Most learning is lost because notes are scattered and disconnected. Ethan OS keeps the original capture, your interpretation, extracted ideas, relationships between ideas, summaries, and reviews in one structured but conversational system.

## What you do

- Mention something you read, watched, heard, or thought about.
- Answer a few follow-up questions about what stood out or how it connects to things you already know.
- Ask for a summary, a connection, or a review later.

## What Ethan OS does

- Stores the raw capture.
- Identifies and saves reusable ideas, separating source claims from your interpretation.
- Links related ideas so old learning resurfaces when relevant.
- Builds personal summaries from your own captures, not generic summaries.
- Schedules lightweight review prompts so useful ideas stick.
- Grounds discussion in the actual source when a legitimate digital copy is available.

## Key ideas

- **Captures are raw; ideas are curated.** Not every note becomes a durable idea.
- **Your position matters.** Ethan OS records whether you agree, disagree, or are still exploring.
- **Sources stay separate.** A book, article, or conversation is a source; your ideas, summaries, and reviews are separate objects linked back to it.
- **Cross-source retrieval.** You can ask what you have learned about a topic across every source you have consumed.

## Example interaction

> **You:** "I listened to a podcast about decision fatigue."  
> **OS:** "What stood out?"  
> **You:** "The idea that small repeated decisions drain willpower for bigger ones."  
> **OS:** "Got it. I'll save that as an idea and link it to any related notes you already have. Want to add where you disagree or where it connects to something else?"

## What gets saved

- **Capture** — your original note or transcript.
- **Source** — the book, article, podcast, course, or conversation.
- **Idea** — a durable, reusable insight extracted from captures.
- **Summary** — your personal synthesis of a source.
- **Review** — a spaced prompt to revisit an idea.
- **Relationships** — typed links between sources, captures, and ideas.

## Important behaviors

- Never fabricate source content.
- Distinguish source claims, your interpretation, and AI-generated explanation.
- Preserve disagreement and uncertainty.
- Do not dump full copyrighted text into storage.
