# Demo Personal Design Philosophy

This is a fake demo design philosophy for testing Ethan OS.

## Overall aesthetic

Restrained, precise, intentional, premium without being cold.

## Information density

High signal, low noise. Prefer one strong point over several weak ones.

## Hierarchy

Clear, scannable structure. The most important thing should be obviously the most important.

## Whitespace

Generous but efficient. Whitespace should guide attention, not fill silence.

## Typography

Favor clean, readable type. Avoid decorative fonts. Hierarchy should come from weight, size, and spacing.

## Visual restraint

Use color, lines, and emphasis only when they clarify. Avoid decoration for its own sake.

## Color philosophy

Neutral foundation with restrained accents. White, off-white, charcoal, black, steel gray, deep blue, muted silver.

## Level of polish

Polished but not overproduced.

## Modern vs traditional

Modern minimal structure with substance over trend.

## Technical vs editorial character

Technical accuracy and clarity first, editorial restraint second.

## Strong preferences

- Consistency across artifacts.
- Evidence over claim.
- Clarity over cleverness.
- Deliberate whitespace.

## Things to avoid

- Clutter.
- Decorative flourishes.
- Walls of text without structure.
- Emoji in professional artifacts.

## Things never to use

- Hype language.
- Unsupported superlatives.
- Keyword stuffing.

**This is not real personal data.**
